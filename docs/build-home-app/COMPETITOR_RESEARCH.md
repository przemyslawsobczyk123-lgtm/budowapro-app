# Competitor and Market Research: BudowaPRO

## Scope

The review covers four product classes, because no single competitor represents the whole homeowner workflow:

1. homeowner build and renovation control,
2. contractor construction management,
3. field documentation and defect tracking,
4. home record, inventory and post-handover maintenance.

The goal is not to copy every feature. The goal is to identify repeated workflows, remove contractor ERP overhead and produce one compact product for a private investor.

## Repeating Market Patterns

The strongest products repeatedly connect five dimensions:

```text
WHAT happened
  + WHEN it happened
  + WHERE it belongs (stage / room / plan position)
  + WHO is responsible
  + PROOF (photo / receipt / protocol / note)
```

The most valuable repeated capabilities are:

- planned, committed, paid and forecast cost states,
- receipt/invoice scan with review before posting,
- tasks, milestones and schedule dependencies,
- contractor quotes, visits, payment schedules and ratings,
- room-level decisions, wish lists and material selections,
- daily logs and change/decision history,
- photos attached to stages, rooms and plan positions,
- punch lists with assignee, deadline, evidence and sign-off,
- exportable reports and a permanent property record,
- offline field capture with later processing or sync.

## Compact Product Decision

BudowaPRO should expose five primary destinations only:

| Tab | User question | Included modules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | What requires my attention today? | health score, risks, visits, quick capture, assistant |
| Plan | What happens next and what must be decided first? | stages, checklist, schedule, diary, decisions |
| Budzet | How much is planned, committed, paid and left? | costs, OCR, quotes, orders, returns, forecast |
| Budowa | Where is the proof and what is hidden in the building? | photos, plans, documents, defects, warranties |
| Wiecej | Where are specialist tools? | rooms, contacts, calculators, reports, home record, settings |

Detailed modules remain separate screens, but they are reached contextually. The mobile navigation does not expose 15 equal-level tabs.

## New Capabilities To Add

### Universal capture inbox

One `+` action accepts:

- receipt/photo/PDF,
- typed note,
- voice note,
- cost,
- task,
- decision,
- defect,
- technical photo.

The result is a draft. User confirms its type and links before it affects the budget or checklist.

### Decision and change log

Every scope change records:

- original decision,
- new decision,
- cost delta,
- schedule delta,
- approving person,
- related quote/document,
- date and reason.

This closes a major gap between a budget tracker and real construction control.

### Plan pins

Photos, defects and installation notes can be pinned to a floor plan or simple room sketch. Full CAD editing is not required for MVP.

### Project health

A transparent health summary uses six dimensions:

- budget,
- schedule,
- critical checklist,
- unresolved decisions,
- missing evidence,
- unpaid/overdue items.

The score must always show its reasons. No black-box grade.

### Home record after handover

The project becomes a permanent home file containing:

- hidden installation map,
- equipment and serial numbers,
- warranties and service dates,
- renovation history,
- receipts and capital improvements,
- final handover/insurance/resale export.

### BudowaPRO Assistant

Two layers:

1. Offline rule engine for stage reminders, missing evidence and deadline conflicts.
2. Optional AI for summaries, suggested questions and document extraction after explicit opt-in.

The assistant may suggest and explain. It may not:

- approve technical work,
- replace a construction manager, inspector or designer,
- diagnose safety-critical defects from a photo as fact,
- change budget totals without confirmation,
- upload project content without a clear data-selection step.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- Contractor CRM and lead pipeline.
- Employee time clock and payroll.
- Full accounting or VAT filing.
- BIM authoring and full CAD editor.
- Legal replacement for the official Electronic Construction Log.
- Public marketplace in the core workflow.
- Mandatory account, backend or cloud upload in MVP.

## Product Position

BudowaPRO is the private investor control center:

- faster than Excel for daily capture,
- more structured than WhatsApp and the phone gallery,
- more construction-aware than generic task boards,
- more compact than contractor ERP,
- more private than cloud-first construction CRMs.
