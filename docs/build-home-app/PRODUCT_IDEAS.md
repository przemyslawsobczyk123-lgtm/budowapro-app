# BudowaPro Product Ideas

## MVP Must Have

- Project dashboard with budget, actual spend and current stage.
- Manual cost entry with stage/category/vendor/date filters.
- Receipt scan with OCR review before saving.
- Stage checklist templates, especially `Stan 0`.
- Contractor/contact list with scheduled visits.
- Attachments for receipts, quotes, invoices, photos and warranties.
- Reports by stage, category, vendor and month.
- CSV/PDF export.
- Local-first privacy: no upload, no account, no document telemetry.

## High-Value Next Features

- Quote comparison: compare three contractor offers by scope, price, exclusions and payment schedule.
- Budget templates by build standard: economic, mid, premium.
- Room-level renovation budgets.
- Material calculator: concrete, rebar, paint, plaster, tiles, panels, insulation, roof area.
- Delivery tracker: ordered, delivered, partial, returned.
- Warranty and return reminders.
- Defect/punch list with photo, owner, due date and status.
- Construction diary with daily note, weather, workers on site and photos.
- Photo evidence sets: before covering foundations, pipes, cables, insulation and waterproofing.
- Cash-flow plan: expected payments by month and stage.
- Bank drawdown package: summary PDF plus selected photos/invoices.
- Local encrypted backup ZIP.
- Import from CSV/Excel to help users migrate existing sheets.
- Room-level renovation mode with budgets, decisions, dimensions and photos per room.
- Material delivery board with ordered/delivered/partial/returned statuses.
- Return tracker for unused materials and refundable deposits.
- Simple calculators for tiles, paint, insulation, concrete and flooring waste.
- Universal capture inbox for photo, receipt, voice, note, task, decision and defect drafts.
- Decision/change register with cost and schedule deltas.
- Project health summary with explicit reasons and direct next actions.
- Floor-plan pins for technical photos, defects, measurements and equipment.
- Permanent home record with equipment, serial numbers, warranties and service reminders.
- Offline rule-based assistant for stage risks and missing evidence.

## Stage Checklist Expansion

### Stan 0

- Geotechnical survey.
- Surveyor stakeout.
- Site access road.
- Temporary power/water.
- Humus removal.
- Excavation level check.
- Foundation reinforcement photos.
- Foundation grounding / bednarka / uziom.
- Utility sleeves: water, power, sewage, gas, internet, gate, garden, heat pump.
- Drainage/waterproofing decision before backfill.
- Concrete delivery docs.
- As-built measurement after foundation.

### SSO

- Wall material delivery and storage.
- Lintels and beams checked.
- Chimney/ventilation routes.
- Roof structure quote and schedule.
- Window measurement timing.
- Rainwater downpipes planned.

### SSZ

- Windows and doors protocol.
- Garage door measurement/install.
- Roof coverage acceptance.
- Temporary security.
- Moisture checks.

### Instalacje

- Electrical circuit plan.
- Extra sockets before plaster.
- External power: gate, garden, terrace, shed, EV charger.
- Low voltage: internet, cameras, alarm, doorbell, intercom.
- Plumbing pressure test.
- Underfloor heating pressure test.
- Ventilation and recuperation routes.
- Photos before plaster/covering.

### Deweloperski

- Plasters acceptance.
- Screed level/moisture.
- Insulation checks.
- Heat source commissioning.
- Stair and railing measurements.
- Bathroom waterproofing before tiles.

### Wykonczenie

- Room-by-room budget.
- Tile, paint, floor and door quantities.
- Delivery dates.
- Returns and leftovers.
- Warranty cards.
- Final defect list.

## Monetization Ideas

- Free: one project, manual costs, basic checklist.
- Premium one-time: unlimited projects, OCR, exports, backup, advanced reports.
- Template packs: renovation, SSO/SSZ, deweloperski, finishing, apartment flip.
- No ads in core workflow; ads would weaken trust in a finance/construction app.

## Product Pushback

- Do not start with collaboration/backend. It adds accounts, sync conflicts, permissions and privacy risk before core value is proven.
- Do not make OCR the center of MVP. A wrong OCR budget is worse than manual entry.
- Do not copy Excel grids directly. Use structured forms, filters and reports, then export if needed.
- Do not store construction advice as hardcoded paragraphs only. Use checklist items with status, evidence, due date and risk.
- Do not expose every module as a primary navigation tab. Keep five stable destinations.
- Do not let an assistant approve work, diagnose safety defects as fact or post financial data automatically.
- Do not claim to replace the official Electronic Construction Log or licensed professionals.
